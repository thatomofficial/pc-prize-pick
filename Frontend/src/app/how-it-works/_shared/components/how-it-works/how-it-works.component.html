SA · §17

Operating under SA competition law.

Every draw is governed by South African competition statutes, audited by an independent firm, and resolved in SA courts. The skill mechanic is Spot-the-Ball — every entry that hits the exact pixel wins (one PC per winner, procured from the wave's entry pool). When nobody is exact, the closest click takes the prize; ties are settled by entry timestamp.
